# Pharma News 2024-2025

## Major FDA Approvals

### 2024
- Donanemab (Kisunla, Eli Lilly): anti-amyloid antibody for early Alzheimer's disease. Approved July 2024. Slows cognitive decline by 35% vs placebo.
- Resmetirom (Rezdiffra, Madrigal): first treatment approved for NASH/MASH (metabolic steatohepatitis). March 2024. Selective thyroid hormone receptor beta agonist.
- Iptacopan (Fabhalta, Novartis): first oral complement factor B inhibitor for paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH). December 2023/2024.
- Crovalimab (Piasky, Roche): subcutaneous anti-C5 for PNH. Once every 4 weeks administration.
- Suzetrigine (Journavx, Vertex): first non-opioid analgesic of a new class (NaV1.8 inhibitor) for acute pain. Approved January 2025.

### Notable Oncology Approvals
- Dato-DXd (datopotamab deruxtecan): anti-TROP2 ADC for non-small cell lung cancer
- Tarlatamab (Imdelltra, Amgen): DLL3xCD3 bispecific antibody for small cell lung cancer
- Ivonescimab: anti-PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody (approved in China)
- Zanidatamab: anti-HER2 bispecific antibody for biliary tract cancers

## Major Trends 2024-2025

### GLP-1 / Obesity Revolution
Semaglutide (Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Zepbound) dominate the exploding obesity market. Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly are in intense competition. The GLP-1 market is projected to exceed $100 billion by 2030. New molecules in development: survodutide (Boehringer), retatrutide (Lilly, GIP/GLP-1/glucagon triple agonist), orforglipron (Lilly, oral GLP-1), amycretin (Novo, oral). Impact beyond diabetes and obesity: proven cardiovascular benefits, ongoing studies in NASH, sleep apnea, heart failure, kidney disease, and even Alzheimer's.

### AI and Drug Discovery
- Isomorphic Labs (DeepMind/Alphabet) signs major deals with Eli Lilly and Novartis
- Recursion acquires Exscientia for $688M (2024) creating an AI pharma giant
- Insilico Medicine advances INS018_055, an AI-discovered molecule, to Phase II for pulmonary fibrosis
- AlphaFold 3 (DeepMind) predicts protein-molecule interactions with unprecedented accuracy
- Major pharma companies are massively integrating AI into their R&D processes

### ADC (Antibody-Drug Conjugates) Boom
ADCs are the fastest-growing therapeutic class in oncology. Over 200 ADCs in clinical development. Enhertu (trastuzumab deruxtecan) generates over $4 billion in sales. New payloads: topoisomerase I inhibitors, MMAE, PBD. New targets: TROP2, Nectin-4, HER3, B7-H3, claudin 18.2.

### Radiopharmaceuticals
Novartis dominates with Pluvicto (Lu-177 PSMA) for prostate cancer. Eli Lilly acquires Point Biopharma, Bristol-Myers Squibb acquires RayzeBio to enter the space. AstraZeneca acquires Fusion Pharmaceuticals. Actinium-225 in development as a more potent alternative to lutetium-177.

## Regulatory and Policy Challenges

### Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) Impact
First Medicare price negotiations concluded in 2024. Price reductions of 38% to 79% on the first 10 negotiated drugs (Eliquis, Jardiance, Xarelto, Januvia, etc.). The pharma industry challenged the law in court without success. Impact on R&D strategies: some companies reducing investment in small molecules (exposed to negotiations earlier than biologics).

### Humira Biosimilars in the US
Launch of multiple Humira (adalimumab) biosimilars in 2023-2024. Prices dropped by 80% for some biosimilars. AbbVie limited erosion through exclusivity contracts with PBMs (Pharmacy Benefit Managers). The US biosimilar market is accelerating with more waves to come (Stelara, Keytruda).

### AI Regulation in Pharma
FDA publishes guidelines on AI use in drug development. EMA creates a dedicated working group. Authorities encourage AI adoption while requiring transparency and scientific validity.
