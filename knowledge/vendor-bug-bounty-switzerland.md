# Bug Bounty Switzerland (BBS) — Leading Swiss Cybersecurity Testing Platform

## Company Overview

Bug Bounty Switzerland AG, commonly known as BBS, is the leading Swiss platform for AI-driven and data-powered security testing. BBS was founded in 2020 and has rapidly evolved from a bug bounty program facilitator into a full technology company that orchestrates ethical hackers — both human and machine — using artificial intelligence. BBS's mission is to redefine security testing globally through its innovative Cyber Resilience Platform.

## Core Products and Services

### 1. Cyber Resilience Shield (CRS)
The flagship product — an AI-powered, always-on, self-learning, plug-and-play security testing solution. Launched in November 2024 as a simple plug-and-play product usable without prior security knowledge, without internal security resources, and at a clearly calculable flat rate. Key features:
- **Semantic Living Digital Twin** — Creates a digital brain of an organization that learns and improves with every test iteration
- **AI Matchmaking** — Ensures the right ethical hackers are activated at the right time, in the right place, with the right task
- **Intelligent Orchestration** — AI-driven coordination of human hackers and AI agents
- **Context-Appropriate Reporting** — Complex technical findings prepared for all levels from developers to the board, with natural language and business relevance
- **Continuous Adaptive Testing** — Security testing strategies based on real-time information, autonomously executed

### 2. Reality Check
An entry-level security assessment where BBS manages everything. In two weeks, organizations receive a comprehensive "Reality Check Report" showing:
- How the hacker engagement proceeded
- Where and how vulnerabilities were found
- Severity assessment of vulnerabilities
- Recommended remediation steps
The service is fully managed (Managed Service), private (invitation-only hackers), within a defined timeframe, and delivers detailed insights.

### 3. Bug Bounty Programs
- **Pilot Programs:** Custom-designed according to individual needs, running several weeks to months
- **Continuous Programs:** Ongoing testing with adjustable parameters, soft-launched with closed groups, scalable
- **Platform License:** Self-service option with access to global community of security researchers and ethical hackers
- **Public Trust Experience:** Full path to public bug bounty program demonstrating security commitment

### 4. Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (VDP)
Formal framework for responsible vulnerability reporting, demonstrating security commitment and legal safe harbor.

### 5. Security Advisories
Published CVEs including CVE-2023-28731, CVE-2023-28732, CVE-2023-28733 — demonstrating responsible disclosure practices.

## Technology and AI Integration

Bug Bounty Switzerland has positioned itself as a technology company since launching the first Swiss bug bounty platform in 2021. AI integration began in 2023 through the Microsoft for Startups program with privileged access to Azure OpenAI.

### AI-Driven Security Testing Flywheel
The platform implements an end-to-end intelligent flywheel: **Scope → Match → Execute → Validate → Learn**
- **Scoping and Planning:** Automatically generated and continuously optimized based on semantic understanding
- **AI Matchmaking:** Context-based matchmaking and orchestration of ethical hackers
- **Agentic AI:** Automation of previously manual processes (Service-as-Software concept)
- **Data Foundation:** Continuously collecting, labeling, and structuring data to unlock potential with AI

### Key Technical Differentiators
- First Swiss-built bug bounty platform (launched 2021)
- Built on Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure
- AI-powered orchestration of human and machine hackers
- Semantic understanding of organizational context
- Prioritized action recommendations in natural language with business relevance

## Relevance for Pharmaceutical Industry

Bug Bounty Switzerland is strategically relevant to the pharmaceutical industry for several reasons:

### Healthcare Sector Expertise
BBS has demonstrated expertise in healthcare cybersecurity through programs with University Hospital Zurich (USZ) and health insurer KPT. Hospital infrastructure is among Switzerland's critical infrastructure, making BBS's experience directly transferable to pharmaceutical companies.

### Regulatory Compliance Support
- Bug bounty programs are officially part of Switzerland's National Cyber Strategy (NCS) since April 2023
- The Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) in the EU increasingly requires proactive security testing
- BBS's Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (VDP) framework supports regulatory compliance requirements
- GxP-regulated pharmaceutical environments can benefit from structured security testing

### Swiss Pharma Hub Proximity
Based in Zurich (The Circle 60 at Zurich Airport), BBS is in the heart of Switzerland's pharmaceutical and biotech ecosystem, close to:
- Roche and Novartis headquarters in Basel (1 hour)
- The broader Swiss pharma manufacturing and R&D corridor
- Swiss regulatory bodies (Swissmedic)

### Critical Infrastructure Protection
Pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities, clinical trial data systems, and drug supply chains are classified as critical infrastructure. BBS has proven expertise in testing critical infrastructure (BKW energy, Swiss Federal Administration, hospital systems).

### Data Protection & IP Security
Pharmaceutical companies handle extremely sensitive data (clinical trial results, drug formulas, patient data). BBS's managed service model with private, invitation-only hacker programs provides confidential security testing appropriate for pharma environments.
