# Pharmaceutical Science

## Major Therapeutic Classes

### Oncology
Oncology is the largest pharma spending category globally (over $200 billion). Therapeutic approaches include:
- Cytotoxic chemotherapy: alkylating agents, antimetabolites, topoisomerase inhibitors
- Targeted therapies: kinase inhibitors (imatinib, osimertinib), PARP inhibitors (olaparib)
- Immunotherapy: immune checkpoint inhibitors (anti-PD-1/PD-L1: pembrolizumab, nivolumab; anti-CTLA-4: ipilimumab)
- ADC (Antibody-Drug Conjugates): trastuzumab deruxtecan (Enhertu), sacituzumab govitecan (Trodelvy)
- CAR-T cell therapies: tisagenlecleucel (Kymriah), axicabtagene ciloleucel (Yescarta)
- Bispecific antibodies: blinatumomab, teclistamab

### Immunology and Inflammation
- Anti-TNF: adalimumab (Humira), infliximab (Remicade), etanercept (Enbrel)
- Anti-IL-17: secukinumab (Cosentyx), ixekizumab (Taltz)
- Anti-IL-23: guselkumab (Tremfya), risankizumab (Skyrizi)
- JAK inhibitors: tofacitinib (Xeljanz), upadacitinib (Rinvoq), baricitinib (Olumiant)
- Anti-IL-4/IL-13: dupilumab (Dupixent) for atopic dermatitis and asthma

### Cardiology and Metabolism
- Statins: atorvastatin, rosuvastatin (LDL-cholesterol reduction)
- PCSK9 inhibitors: evolocumab (Repatha), alirocumab (Praluent)
- Direct oral anticoagulants (DOAC): rivaroxaban (Xarelto), apixaban (Eliquis)
- GLP-1 agonists: semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy), tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound)
- SGLT2 inhibitors: empagliflozin (Jardiance), dapagliflozin (Farxiga) - diabetes and heart failure
- Inclisiran (Leqvio): siRNA targeting PCSK9, twice-yearly administration

### Neurology
- Anti-amyloid for Alzheimer's: lecanemab (Leqembi), donanemab (Kisunla) - monoclonal antibodies targeting amyloid plaques
- Anti-CGRP for migraine: erenumab (Aimovig), fremanezumab (Ajovy), galcanezumab (Emgality)
- SMA gene therapies: onasemnogene abeparvovec (Zolgensma), nusinersen (Spinraza)
- Antisense and siRNA: patisiran (Onpattro) for transthyretin amyloidosis

### Rare (Orphan) Diseases
Over 7,000 rare diseases identified, affecting 300 million people worldwide. Regulatory incentives: Orphan Drug Act (US), European orphan drug regulation. Prices often exceed $100,000 per year. Examples: ivacaftor (Kalydeco) for cystic fibrosis, nusinersen (Spinraza) for SMA.

## Emerging Therapeutic Modalities

### mRNA (Messenger RNA)
Technology validated by COVID-19 vaccines. Principles: injection of mRNA encoding a therapeutic protein, protein production by patient cells. Pipeline: flu, RSV, CMV, HIV vaccines, personalized cancer vaccines. Advantages: rapid development, standardized manufacturing. Challenges: stability, delivery, immunogenicity.

### siRNA and Antisense Oligonucleotides (ASO)
- siRNA: small interfering RNAs that degrade target mRNA. Examples: patisiran, givosiran, inclisiran
- ASO: antisense oligonucleotides that modulate gene expression. Examples: nusinersen, eteplirsen
- GalNAc conjugation: hepatic delivery technology revolutionizing the field (Alnylam)

### CRISPR Genome Editing
- CRISPR-Cas9: molecular scissors enabling precise genome modification
- First approval: exagamglogene autotemcel (Casgevy) for sickle cell disease and beta-thalassemia (2023)
- Pipeline: genetic diseases, cancer, cardiovascular diseases
- Key players: CRISPR Therapeutics, Editas Medicine, Intellia Therapeutics
- Base editing and prime editing: more precise versions of CRISPR

### Antibody-Drug Conjugates (ADC)
Combination of an antibody targeting a tumor cell with a cytotoxic payload. Connected via a chemical linker. Enables delivery of chemotherapy directly to cancer cells. Rapidly growing market: Enhertu (Daiichi Sankyo/AstraZeneca), Padcev, Adcetris. Over 100 ADCs in clinical development.

### Radiopharmaceuticals
Targeted molecules coupled with radioactive isotopes for diagnosis (imaging) or treatment (internal radiotherapy). Examples: lutetium-177-PSMA (Pluvicto, Novartis) for prostate cancer, actinium-225. Rapidly expanding market.

## Clinical Trials

### Modern Trial Designs
- Adaptive trials: protocol modifications during the study based on interim analyses
- Basket trials: test a treatment across different cancer types sharing a common mutation
- Umbrella trials: test different treatments on a single cancer type stratified by biomarkers
- Platform trials: permanent infrastructure sequentially testing multiple treatments
- Decentralized clinical trials (DCT): remote trials using digital technologies

### Biomarkers and Companion Diagnostics
A biomarker is a measurable biological indicator that predicts treatment response. A companion diagnostic (CDx) is a mandatory test before drug prescription. Examples: HER2 test for trastuzumab, PD-L1 test for pembrolizumab, BRCA test for olaparib.

### Real-World Evidence (RWE)
Data collected outside clinical trials (electronic health records, registries, claims data). Growing use by regulatory authorities to complement clinical trial data. FDA and EMA publish guidelines on RWE use.

## Pharmaceutical Manufacturing

### Small Molecules
Multi-step chemical synthesis. Manufacturing of API (Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient) followed by formulation (tablets, capsules, injectables). Main production sites: India, China, Ireland, Puerto Rico.

### Biologics
Production by cell culture (CHO cells) or microbial fermentation. Bioreactors of 2,000 to 20,000 liters. Downstream processing: purification by chromatography, filtration, formulation. More complex and costly manufacturing than small molecules. Fill-finish: filling under aseptic conditions.

### Cell and Gene Therapies
Autologous (patient-specific) or allogeneic (universal donor) manufacturing. Complex logistics: cold chain, rapid transport, individual traceability. Viral vectors (AAV, lentivirus) produced in cell culture. Limited scalability, very high production costs.
