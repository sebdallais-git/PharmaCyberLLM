# Top Phase 3 Drug Candidates and Pipeline (2025-2026)

## Overview

The global pharmaceutical Phase 3 pipeline in 2025-2026 is dominated by next-generation obesity therapies, precision oncology, Alzheimer's disease-modifying treatments, gene/cell therapies, and immunology breakthroughs. This document covers the most anticipated late-stage candidates with blockbuster potential.

---

## Obesity and Metabolic Disease

### Retatrutide (Eli Lilly)
- **Class**: First-in-class GIP/GLP-1/glucagon triagonist
- **Indication**: Obesity, type 2 diabetes
- **Phase 2 data**: 24% weight loss over 48 weeks (best-in-class)
- **Phase 3**: TRIUMPH program — TRIUMPH-5 readout expected late 2026, seven additional Phase III readouts expected in 2026 including maintenance dosing studies
- **Peak sales estimate**: $2 billion+ by 2030
- **Significance**: Triple agonist mechanism could surpass current GLP-1 therapies

### Orforglipron (Eli Lilly)
- **Class**: Non-peptide oral GLP-1 receptor agonist
- **Indication**: Obesity, type 2 diabetes
- **Phase 3 data**: 7.5-11.2% weight loss across dosing groups
- **Approval expected**: Late 2026
- **Significance**: First oral GLP-1 — eliminates need for injections, massive market expansion potential

### CagriSema (Novo Nordisk)
- **Class**: Cagrilintide (amylin analog) + semaglutide (GLP-1) combination
- **Indication**: Obesity, type 2 diabetes
- **Phase IIIa data**: 13.7% mean weight loss at Week 68
- **Approval filing**: Expected early 2026
- **Significance**: Next-gen combination from the Ozempic/Wegovy maker

### MariTide (Amgen)
- **Class**: Bispecific antibody targeting GIP receptor and activin receptor
- **Indication**: Obesity, atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, heart failure, obstructive sleep apnea
- **Phase 3**: Six global Phase III studies underway as of November 2025
- **Significance**: Monthly dosing (vs. weekly for semaglutide), novel mechanism

## Oncology

### Imlunestrant (Eli Lilly)
- **Class**: Oral selective estrogen receptor degrader (SERD)
- **Indication**: ER+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer
- **Phase 3**: Positive results, FDA approval expected late 2025/early 2026
- **Significance**: Oral alternative to injectable fulvestrant

### Camizestrant (AstraZeneca)
- **Class**: Next-generation oral SERD
- **Indication**: ER+/HER2- breast cancer
- **Peak sales estimate**: ~$2 billion by 2030
- **Significance**: Blockbuster potential in the largest breast cancer subtype

### Ivonescimab (Summit/Akeso)
- **Class**: PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody
- **Indication**: EGFR-mutant non-small cell lung cancer
- **Significance**: May offer chemotherapy-free alternative for lung cancer patients

### Daraxonrasib (Revolution Medicines)
- **Class**: RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor
- **Indication**: Second-line pancreatic cancer (KRAS mutations)
- **Significance**: Targets multiple RAS mutations in hard-to-treat pancreatic cancer

### Iberdomide and Mezigdomide (Bristol Myers Squibb)
- **Class**: Next-generation oral CELMoD agents
- **Indication**: Multiple myeloma
- **Significance**: Aim to replace older myeloma therapies with improved efficacy

### Karyopharm Pipeline
- **XPORT-EC-042**: Phase 3 in endometrial cancer, top-line data expected mid-2026
- **SENTRY**: Phase 3 in myelofibrosis, top-line data expected March 2026

## Neurology and Alzheimer's Disease

### Alzheimer's Pipeline Overview
The 2025 Alzheimer's drug development pipeline hosts 182 trials and 138 novel drugs addressing 15 basic disease processes.

### Remternetug (Eli Lilly)
- **Class**: Anti-amyloid antibody
- **Indication**: Early Alzheimer's disease
- **Phase 3**: TRAILRUNNER-ALZ 1 top-line data expected 2026
- **Significance**: Potential best-in-class anti-amyloid with subcutaneous dosing

### KarXT / Cobenfy (Karuna/BMS)
- **Class**: Muscarinic receptor agonist
- **Indication**: Alzheimer's-related psychosis
- **Phase 3**: Two ongoing trials (380 and 400 participants), results expected 2025-2026
- **Note**: Already approved for schizophrenia (Cobenfy)

### ACI-24.060 (AC Immune/Takeda)
- **Class**: Active Aβ vaccine
- **Indication**: Alzheimer's disease
- **Phase 2**: Interim results expected early 2026
- **Significance**: Vaccine approach could offer more accessible treatment

### Oveporexton (Takeda)
- **Class**: Orexin receptor-2 agonist
- **Indication**: Narcolepsy type 1
- **Phase 3**: Met all primary and secondary endpoints in two positive trials
- **NDA submission**: Planned late 2025
- **Peak sales estimate**: $2-3 billion
- **Significance**: First-in-class wake-promoting agent, blockbuster potential

## Cardiovascular

### Pelacarsen (Novartis)
- **Class**: Antisense oligonucleotide targeting Lp(a)
- **Indication**: Lp(a)-driven cardiovascular disease
- **Phase 3**: Event-driven trial, data anticipated 2026
- **Significance**: First therapy targeting elevated Lp(a), affects ~20% of population

## Immunology and Nephrology

### Povetacicept (Vertex)
- **Class**: BAFF/APRIL dual inhibitor
- **Indication**: IgA nephropathy
- **Phase 3**: Interim analysis planned early 2026, could lead to accelerated approval
- **Significance**: Potential best-in-class for IgA nephropathy

### Obinutuzumab (Roche)
- **Class**: Anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody
- **Indication**: Lupus nephritis
- **Phase 3 (REGENCY)**: 46.4% complete renal response at 76 weeks vs. 33.1% placebo
- **Significance**: New treatment option for severe lupus

## Gene Therapy, CRISPR, and Cell Therapy

### Exa-cel / Casgevy (CRISPR Therapeutics/Vertex)
- **Class**: CRISPR/Cas9 gene editing therapy
- **Indication**: Sickle cell disease and beta-thalassemia (approved), now in pediatric Phase 3
- **Status**: Enrollment completed in pediatric Phase 3 studies
- **Significance**: First approved CRISPR therapy, expanding to pediatric population

### CTX112 (CRISPR Therapeutics)
- **Class**: Allogeneic anti-CD19 CAR-T
- **Indication**: Autoimmune disease and hematologic malignancies
- **Status**: RMAT designation from FDA, clinical trials ongoing
- **Significance**: Off-the-shelf CAR-T for autoimmune disease — potential paradigm shift

### Tab-cel (Atara Biotherapeutics)
- **Class**: Allogeneic T-cell immunotherapy
- **Indication**: EBV+ post-transplant lymphoproliferative disease
- **Status**: BLA resubmission accepted with Priority Review, PDUFA January 2026
- **Significance**: First allogeneic T-cell therapy if approved

### Deramiocel / CAP-1002 (Capricor Therapeutics)
- **Class**: Allogeneic cardiosphere-derived cell therapy
- **Indication**: Duchenne muscular dystrophy cardiomyopathy
- **Status**: Priority Review, PDUFA August 2025
- **Significance**: First cell therapy for DMD cardiomyopathy

### MCO-010 / Sonpiretigene (Nanoscope Therapeutics)
- **Class**: Ambient-light activatable gene therapy
- **Indication**: Retinitis pigmentosa
- **Status**: Rolling BLA submission, completion expected early 2026
- **Significance**: Novel light-activatable approach for inherited blindness

## Infectious Disease

### Bemnifosbuvir + Ruzasvir (Atea Pharmaceuticals)
- **Class**: Direct-acting antiviral combination
- **Indication**: Hepatitis C (HCV)
- **Phase 3**: First topline results expected mid-2026
- **Significance**: Potential pan-genotypic cure with shorter treatment duration

## Psychedelics and Mental Health

### COMP360 Psilocybin (Compass Pathways)
- **Class**: Synthetic psilocybin
- **Indication**: Treatment-resistant depression
- **Phase 3**: COMP005 (26-week data) and COMP006 (585 participants) results expected early 2026
- **Significance**: Could become first FDA-approved psilocybin therapy
