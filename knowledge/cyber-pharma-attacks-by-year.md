# Pharma Cyber Attacks Timeline by Year

## 2024-2025

### 2024 Notable Incidents
1. Change Healthcare (ALPHV/BlackCat ransomware) - $2.87B cost, 100M+ records, US pharmacy system disrupted for weeks
2. Cencora data breach - Patient data from multiple major pharma companies stolen
3. Hoya Corporation (Hunters International ransomware) - Production halted
4. Octapharma Plasma ransomware - 190+ plasma donation centers closed temporarily
5. Henry Schein (second attack) - BlackCat ransomware re-attack after initial October 2023 incident
6. Rite Aid data breach - 2.2M customer records stolen via phishing
7. WebTPA (pharma benefits administrator) - 2.5M individuals' data breached
8. Ascension Health (major hospital/pharmacy system) - Black Basta ransomware, widespread disruption
9. Synnovis (UK pathology/pharma lab services) - Ransomware caused London hospital crisis
10. OneBlood (blood supply for pharma) - Ransomware disrupted blood supply chain
11. LoanDepot/pharmacy benefit crossover attacks targeting prescription data
12. McLaren Health Care - ALPHV ransomware affecting pharmacy operations, 2.2M records
