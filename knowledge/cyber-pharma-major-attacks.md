# Major Cyber Attacks on the Pharmaceutical Industry

## Landmark Attacks

### NotPetya Attack on Merck (2017)
- Company: Merck & Co (MSD)
- Date: June 27, 2017
- Attack type: Destructive wiper malware disguised as ransomware (NotPetya)
- Vector: Supply chain attack via Ukrainian tax software MeDoc update
- Impact: 40,000+ computers destroyed, manufacturing halted across multiple sites, unable to fulfill orders for weeks
- Cost: $1.4 billion (Merck's disclosed figure), including $670M in lost sales, $400M+ in remediation
- Time to remediation: 18+ months for full recovery, partial operations restored in 2 weeks
- Insurance: Merck won landmark $1.4B insurance case (2022) after insurers argued "act of war" exclusion
- Key lesson: Collateral damage from nation-state attacks can devastate pharma operations

### Novartis Data Breach (2022)
- Company: Novartis
- Date: June 2022
- Attack type: Data breach and dark web data sale
- Vector: Industrial Spy ransomware group claimed access to internal data
- Impact: Manufacturing and laboratory data reportedly stolen
- Cost: Undisclosed, estimated $50-100M including investigation and remediation
- Time to remediation: 3-6 months

### AstraZeneca Targeted by North Korean Hackers (2020)
- Company: AstraZeneca
- Date: November 2020
- Attack type: Spear-phishing and social engineering
- Vector: North Korean (Lazarus Group) hackers posed as recruiters on LinkedIn, sent malicious documents
- Target: COVID-19 vaccine research data
- Impact: Attack was detected and blocked, no breach confirmed
- Cost: Undisclosed investigation costs
- Time to remediation: Immediate detection, weeks of investigation
- Key lesson: Nation-state actors target vaccine IP during pandemics

### Pfizer/BioNTech COVID-19 Vaccine Data Theft (2020)
- Company: European Medicines Agency (EMA), affecting Pfizer/BioNTech
- Date: December 2020
- Attack type: Targeted intrusion on regulatory agency
- Vector: Unauthorized access to EMA servers
- Impact: COVID-19 vaccine regulatory documents stolen and leaked, some manipulated before release to sow distrust
- Cost: Undisclosed, significant reputational damage
- Time to remediation: Several months for EMA

### Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ransomware (2020)
- Company: Dr. Reddy's Laboratories
- Date: October 2020
- Attack type: Ransomware
- Vector: Unknown initial access
- Impact: Shut down plants in Brazil, India, Russia, UK, and US. Isolated all data center services.
- Cost: Estimated $20-50M in lost production and remediation
- Time to remediation: 2-3 weeks for primary operations
- Context: Occurred just after Dr. Reddy's received approval to conduct trials for Russia's Sputnik V vaccine

### Roper St. Francis Healthcare / Pharmaceutical Supply Chain (2020)

- Cost: $3-5M estimated including legal settlements

### Change Healthcare / UnitedHealth (2024)
- Company: Change Healthcare (UnitedHealth subsidiary), critical pharma payment and claims processor
- Date: February 2024
- Attack type: ALPHV/BlackCat ransomware
- Vector: Compromised credentials on Citrix remote access without MFA
- Impact: Largest healthcare data breach in US history. Disrupted pharmacy claims processing across the US for weeks. 100+ million individuals affected. Pharmacies couldn't process insurance claims.
- Cost: UnitedHealth estimated total cost of $2.87 billion (as of Q3 2024 disclosure)
- Ransom: $22 million reportedly paid
- Time to remediation: Core pharmacy services restored in ~2 weeks, full systems took 4+ months
- Key lesson: Single points of failure in healthcare supply chain create systemic risk

### Anthem Health Insurance Breach (2015) - Pharma Supply Chain Impact
- Company: Anthem Inc (health insurer processing pharma claims)
- Date: February 2015
- Attack type: APT data breach (attributed to China-linked Deep Panda)
- Impact: 78.8 million records stolen including names, SSNs, medical IDs. Massive impact on pharma benefit claims.
- Cost: $115 million settlement + $260M+ in remediation
- Time to remediation: Years of ongoing monitoring

- Company: Apria Healthcare (home pharmacy services)
- Date: Disclosed 2023, breach occurred 2019-2021

### Indian Pharmaceutical Industry Attacks (2020-2023)
Multiple Indian pharmaceutical companies were targeted during and after COVID-19:
- Bharat Biotech: Targeted by APT groups seeking Covaxin data (2021)
- Serum Institute of India: Cyber espionage attempts targeting vaccine production data (2021)
- Cipla: Ransomware attack impacting operations (2022)
- Glenmark Pharmaceuticals: Data breach and ransomware (2020)
- Wockhardt: Targeted attack disrupting operations (2022)
- Estimated combined cost: $200-500M across Indian pharma sector 2020-2023
