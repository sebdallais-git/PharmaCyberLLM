# Pharmaceutical Business

## Business Models

### Blockbuster Model
Historical strategy aiming to develop drugs generating over one billion dollars in annual sales. Classic examples: Lipitor (atorvastatin, Pfizer, peak $13B), Humira (adalimumab, AbbVie, peak $21B). This model is under pressure due to patent expiration and generic/biosimilar competition.

### Specialty Pharma
Model focused on high-value niche therapies for small patient populations. High unit prices, specialized sales forces. Areas: oncology, rare diseases, immunology. Gross margin often above 80%.

### Platform Model
Companies building a technology platform applicable to multiple indications. Examples: Moderna (mRNA platform), BioNTech (mRNA), CRISPR Therapeutics (genome editing). Enables rapid pipeline diversification from a common technology base.

## Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A)

### Recent Mega-Deals
- Pfizer acquires Seagen for $43B (2023) to strengthen oncology pipeline with ADCs (antibody-drug conjugates)
- AbbVie acquires ImmunoGen for $10.1B (2024) for ADCs
- Merck acquires Prometheus Biosciences for $10.8B (2023) for immunology
- Amgen acquires Horizon Therapeutics for $27.8B (2023) for rare diseases
- AstraZeneca acquires Alexion for $39B (2021) for rare diseases

### Strategic Logic Behind M&A
Big pharma acquires biotechs to offset revenue loss from patent expirations (patent cliff). Most sought-after areas: oncology, immunology, rare diseases, neurology. Acquisition premiums range from 30% to 100% over stock price.

## Patent Cliff

### Major Patent Losses 2024-2030
- Humira (AbbVie): biosimilars launched in 2023 in the US, estimated loss of $10B+ in revenue
- Keytruda (Merck): patent expires 2028, represents $25B annual sales
- Opdivo (BMS): patents expiring progressively 2026-2030
- Eliquis (BMS/Pfizer): patent expires 2026, $12B in sales
- Stelara (J&J): biosimilars expected 2025, $10B in sales

### Strategies to Address the Patent Cliff
- Pipeline diversification through M&A
- Life cycle management: new formulations, new indications
- Pivot to biologics and complex therapies (harder to copy)
- Geographic expansion to emerging markets

## Pricing and Market Access

### Pricing Models
- Value-based pricing: price justified by clinical benefit delivered
- International reference pricing: benchmarking against prices in other countries
- Outcome-based contracts: reimbursement linked to real clinical outcomes
- Differential pricing by market: prices adapted to each country's purchasing power

### Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) - USA
US law passed in 2022 allowing Medicare to negotiate prices of certain drugs. First round of negotiations in 2023-2024. Significant impact on pharma revenues in the US. Drugs affected: Eliquis, Jardiance, Xarelto, Januvia, among others.

### European Reimbursement Systems
- France: ASMR (Improvement in Medical Benefit) determines price negotiated with CEPS
- Germany: AMNOG evaluation by G-BA followed by price negotiations
- UK: cost-effectiveness assessment by NICE with threshold of GBP 20-30K per QALY
- Spain and Italy: reference systems and regional negotiations

## Geographic Markets

### Global Pharma Market
The global pharmaceutical market exceeds $1,500 billion in 2024. The US represents about 40% of the global market, followed by Europe (25%), China (10%), Japan (6%).

### Emerging Markets
China is the second-largest market with rapid growth. China's VBP (Volume-Based Procurement) program has sharply reduced generic prices. India is the world's largest generic drug manufacturer. Brazil, Russia, and Middle Eastern countries offer growth opportunities.

## Generics and Biosimilars

### Generics Market
Represents approximately 90% of prescriptions in the US but only 20% of spending. Generic competition can reduce prices by 80-90% after entry of multiple competitors. Major players: Teva, Sandoz, Mylan (Viatris), Sun Pharma, Aurobindo.

### Biosimilars Market
Rapid growth in Europe (mature market) and the US (accelerating since 2023). Biosimilars offer price reductions of 15-40% compared to the reference biologic. Major players: Sandoz, Amgen Biosimilars, Samsung Bioepis, Celltrion, Fresenius Kabi.
